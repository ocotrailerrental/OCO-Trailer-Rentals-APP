# OCO Trailer Rentals — Rental Policy (Draft for Owner Review)

Status: **Draft only — not published or linked from the website**

This document is an operational draft, not legal advice. OCO should have its insurer and a Nebraska/Alaska-licensed attorney review it before publication.

## 1. Eligibility and authorized drivers

Renters and every authorized driver must present a current driver’s license, meet OCO’s minimum age and insurance requirements, and use a tow vehicle rated for the rented trailer and load. The renter is responsible for confirming hitch, brake-controller, wiring, payload, towing, and load-distribution compatibility.

## 2. Reservation and payment

A submitted request is not confirmed until OCO approves it. Rates, taxes, delivery charges, deposits, discounts, and the payment schedule shown on the confirmed reservation control. Discount codes have no cash value and may be limited by date, location, rental length, or use count. OCO currently collects no card number through the website.

## 3. Pickup, return, and inspections

The renter and OCO must document trailer condition at pickup and return. For contactless handoffs, the renter must upload clear, time-stamped views requested in the app and report existing or new damage. The renter should not move an unsafe trailer and must contact OCO if lights, tires, brakes, hitch, safety chains, ramps, or tie-down equipment appear damaged or inoperable.

## 4. Permitted use

The trailer may be used only for lawful transport within its published ratings. Prohibited uses include overloading, racing, towing by an incompatible vehicle, transporting hazardous materials unless OCO expressly approves it in writing, sub-renting, use by an unauthorized driver, or use while impaired.

## 5. Loading and road safety

The renter is responsible for load placement, securement, tongue weight, tie-down inspection, speed, clearance, route selection, and compliance with all laws. Straps and chains must be checked shortly after departure and regularly during travel.

## 6. Damage, loss, tolls, and citations

The renter must promptly report collisions, theft, breakdowns, citations, and damage, preserve evidence, cooperate with OCO and insurers, and obtain a police report where required. Subject to applicable law and the signed agreement, the renter may be responsible for repair, loss of use, towing, recovery, cleaning, missing equipment, tolls, citations, and administrative costs.

## 7. Late return, extension, and abandonment

Extensions require OCO’s written approval before the scheduled return. Late returns may interfere with another renter and may result in additional charges stated in the signed agreement. A trailer must be returned only to the authorized location and must never be abandoned.

## 8. Cancellation and refunds

The final policy should state clear cancellation windows, weather treatment, no-show rules, and refund timing for each market. **Owner decision required before publication.**

## 9. Breakdown and emergency procedure

For immediate danger, move to a safe place if possible and call emergency services. Do not authorize repairs or towing beyond emergency safety measures without OCO approval. OCO contact numbers and after-hours instructions must be inserted here before publication.

## 10. Privacy and inspection photos

Inspection photos and reservation records are private operational records. They may be viewed by the renter and OCO personnel authorized for the applicable location, plus administrators and owners with company-wide responsibility. OCO should publish a separate privacy notice covering retention, deletion, and lawful disclosures.
